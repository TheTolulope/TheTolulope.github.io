Hospital GRC Risk Assessment

Completed

A full NIST SP 800-30 information security risk assessment for the same fictional 350-bed hospital from "Beyond Our Walls" — this project answers how bad the exposure actually is. Built a 15-risk register scored by likelihood × impact, mapped to the HIPAA Security Rule, NIST CSF 2.0, and HHS 405(d) HICP. Found 2 critical and 11 high risks before treatment (down to zero after), tested 14 safeguards against HIPAA/NIST (only 1 fully implemented), and tiered 13 sample vendors by risk — flagging a building HVAC contractor as high-risk purely from network access, despite holding no patient data.
